1. Intelligence

Intelligence is the ability to learn, understand, think, solve problems and make decisions.

Example: A student solving a maths problem.

2. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI is a branch of Computer Science that makes machines perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples: ChatGPT, Siri, self-driving cars.

3. State Space Problems

A State Space Problem is an AI problem where we move from an initial state to a goal state using different actions.

Main components:

- Initial State – Starting point.
- Goal State – Final target.
- States – Possible situations.
- Operators – Actions.
- Constraints – Rules to follow.

4. Types of State Space Problems

A. Water Jug Problem

- Two jugs: 4L and 3L.
- Goal: Measure exactly 2L.
- Actions: Fill, empty and pour.
- Initial State: (0,0).
- Goal State: (2,0).

B. Missionaries and Cannibals (Priests and Cannibals)

- Three missionaries and three cannibals must cross a river.
- Boat carries maximum two people.
- Cannibals must not outnumber missionaries on either bank when missionaries are present.
- Initial State: (3,3,L).
- Goal State: (0,0,R).

C. 8-Puzzle Problem

- Contains 8 numbered tiles and one empty space.
- Goal: Arrange numbers from 1 to 8.
- Actions: Move empty space up, down, left or right.
- Initial State: Random arrangement.
- Goal State: Ordered arrangement.
